# Who joblist is for

## An organization

A company, a cooperative, a nonprofit, a project: wants its jobs where people can find them,
from its own account, and on its own website.
- Makes its domain its Bluesky handle, signs in, describes itself and posts its jobs.
- Its page is `joblist.today/{domain}`; its jobs show on its website with one line to paste.
- Applications go straight to it: a link or an email it gives.

## Someone looking at its jobs

Arrives from a link (its website, its page, a shared job): reads the offer and applies where
it says. Nothing to sign in to, nothing kept.

## Another app

Reads the same records from the organization's account: `today.joblist.company` and
`today.joblist.job` ([docs/lexicons.md](lexicons.md)), and /llms.txt.
